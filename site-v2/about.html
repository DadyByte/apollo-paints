ABOUT US

Apollo Paints

Apollo Paints manufactures industrial coatings, speciality resins and wood finishes from our facility in Karnal, Haryana. We formulate chemical solutions that protect metal substrates and enhance surfaces — available all over India with direct supply to manufacturers, fabricators and dealers.

From heavy-duty industrial applications through to fine wood finishing, our polymer and epoxy formulations are built to resist corrosion, handle demanding environments, and apply cleanly on a production line — backed by reliable nationwide logistics.
